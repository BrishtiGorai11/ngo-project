HOW IT STARTED

From Compassion To Action.

LifeDrop NGO began with a group of people who believed that helping others should not be difficult.

We started by organizing small blood donation activities and connecting willing donors with people in urgent need. Today, our mission continues to grow with every volunteer and every supporter.
